What we stand for

The Brotherhood
Manifesto

We are the V-Twin Brotherhood. We didn't form to be the biggest crew — we formed to be the best one to ride with.

Los Santos is a warzone of griefers and randoms who take without giving. We built a different kind of operation — one where every member is family, and every mission is run with discipline.

The road is long and Los Santos never sleeps. But no V-Twin brother rides alone.

I
Loyalty
Your brothers come first. We defend each other in session, we back each other outside it. No one is left behind on these streets.
II
Code
We operate with a code. No griefing allies, no toxicity, no power tripping. Respect is the currency of this club.
III
Action
We don't wait for others to fix sessions. We handle griefers, protect members, and run operations with precision.

The Code

Brotherhood
Rules

01
Brothers Before AllIf a member calls for backup, you answer. We don't leave brothers hanging — in session or in life.
02
No Friendly FireNever attack a fellow member without consent. Club vs club sparring in private sessions only.
03
Respect is Non-NegotiableZero tolerance for harassment or toxicity toward members, allies, or newcomers. You wear the patch.
04
Stay ActiveAt least one session per week. Life happens — just let us know if you're going quiet. Ghost drops get removed.
05
Be on DiscordNot required every session, but Discord is the clubhouse. Check in, coordinate, be present.
06
No GriefingWe are the counter to griefers — not one of them. No destroying business stock, no trolling randoms unprovoked.
07
Earn Your PatchProspects ride for 2 weeks minimum before full patch. Show loyalty, contribute. The patch means something here.
